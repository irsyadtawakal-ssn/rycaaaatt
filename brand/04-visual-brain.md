# Visual Brain

Oct 5, 2026 · @DEV SS · updated Oct 6, 2026

## How to use this document

This is the visual rulebook for every image, carousel, cover and video made for **@irsyadtawakal**. Follow it literally: when a choice is not covered here, pick the plainer option, never the more decorative one.

The brand in one sentence: a deep indigo canvas, big white headlines in Bricolage Grotesque, one orange word that grabs the eye, and lots of empty space. It should feel captivating, clean and clear, like a smart friend explaining AI, not a tech company selling it.

**Mood:** late-night clarity — a calm, dark room where one idea suddenly lights up.

The six rules that are never broken:

1. Three colours only: Deep Indigo `#261A66`, White `#FFFFFF`, Signal Orange `#EF5F18`.
2. Three fonts only: Bricolage Grotesque (headlines), Figtree (everything else) and Instrument Serif Italic (cover kickers, short slide labels and pause-slide quotes only).
3. Default background is Deep Indigo.
4. Every headline has exactly one word in orange.
5. Orange is only ever large and bold. Never small text, never on a white background (the one exception is a single large bold word on the pause slide).
6. **@irsyadtawakal** appears on every slide (top-left corner label on covers and inner slides) and for the whole length of every video.

Instruction line for any AI tool: *"Follow this Visual Brain exactly: 3 colours, 3 fonts (serif italic only for cover kickers, short slide labels and pause quotes), indigo background, one orange word per headline, left-aligned inner slides with generous empty space (centred magazine layout on covers), and @irsyadtawakal on every frame."*

Source: the Brand Guide of 28 Sep 2026. Visual references: 7 Pinterest boards reviewed on 5 Oct 2026. Their shared direction is a dark base, one hot accent, black-and-white people and oversized type. The layouts below are still defaults to test and refine.

## 1. Palette

Deep Indigo is the room, White is the voice, Orange is the spark. Keep the 60 / 30 / 10 split on every single frame, not just across a whole carousel.

| Role | Name | Hex | Share of each frame | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Deep Indigo | `#261A66` | \~60% | Background of every slide and video overlay. Text colour on the pause slide and on orange blocks. |
| Secondary | White | `#FFFFFF` | \~30% | Headlines, body text, handle, icons, line work and graphic shapes. |
| Accent | Signal Orange | `#EF5F18` | \~10% | The one key word in each headline, big step numbers, arrows, underline bars, buttons, one highlighted icon. |

**Contrast (calculated from the hex codes):** White on Indigo is about 15:1, safe at any size. Orange on Indigo is about 4.5:1, so it is used only for large bold text and shapes. White on Orange is about 3.3:1, so text on an orange button must be large and bold.

**Primary — Deep Indigo**

- Fill the full canvas edge to edge. It is a flat colour: no texture, pattern or vignette, and no gradient. Two exceptions: covers, whose imagery is generated freely, and graphic slides, which may use one very subtle gradient between #261A66 and the tint #3A2A9C (see Layout library).
- **Two allowed tints of Deep Indigo, only where specified:** Indigo Lift `#3A2A9C` (the gradient on graphic slides) and Pause White `#F4F2FF` (the background of the pause slide). They are tints, not new colours, and never appear as text colour.
- On the pause slide and on orange blocks, Deep Indigo becomes the text colour.

**Secondary — White**

- Standard text colour on indigo for headlines, subtext, body and the handle.
- May be used at reduced opacity, which is the same colour, not a new one: 70% for supporting labels such as the slide counter, 15% for divider lines and card outlines. Never below 70% for anything meant to be read.
- White cards are retired: prompts are plain text with an orange bar (section 4), and examples are shown on the slide itself.
- A full off-white background (Pause White #F4F2FF) is allowed on at most one slide per carousel, as a deliberate pause such as myth versus fact or a single key quote. On that slide, text is Deep Indigo and orange appears only as shapes or as one large bold word (120 px or more), never as a label or small text. Use it for the one key insight, usually right before the payoff slide.

**Accent — Signal Orange**

- Exactly one orange word per headline. If two words feel important, choose one.
- Orange shapes: solid arrow (→), thick underline bar under a word (height about 12 px on a 1080 px canvas), solid circle behind a step number, button fill.
- Never for body text, captions under 48 px, the handle, thin lines or long phrases.
- Never orange text on white, except the one large bold word on the pause slide. Never fill more than about 10% of a frame with orange, except on colour-block slides, where an orange block may fill up to 40% of the frame height.

**Exception:** real screenshots of AI tools keep their own native colours. They are evidence, not decoration, and always sit inside a frame on the indigo background. On covers, imagery is free and its colours do not count. Inner slides use designed graphics, not photos.

## 2. Typography

Bricolage Grotesque shouts the hook, Figtree explains it, and Instrument Serif Italic adds one quiet editorial voice on covers. The "accent" in type is a role, not an extra font: it is made with Figtree Medium for small labels and with orange Bricolage for emphasis.

Sizes below are for a 1080 px wide canvas (1080 × 1350, 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1920). For other canvases, scale every size by the canvas width divided by 1080.

| Role | Font | Weight | Size (1080 wide) | Line height | Letter spacing | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display: headline | Bricolage Grotesque | ExtraBold 800 | 120–150 px | 1.0 | −1% to −2% | White, one word Orange |
| Display: big step number | Bricolage Grotesque | ExtraBold 800 | 180–240 px | 1.0 | −2% | Orange |
| Body: subtext | Figtree | Bold 700 | 48–56 px | 1.2 | 0 | White |
| Body: paragraph | Figtree | Regular 400 or Medium 500 | 36–44 px | 1.4 | 0 | White (Deep Indigo on off-white or orange blocks) |
| Accent: handle, labels, slide counter | Figtree | Medium 500 | 30–34 px | 1.2 | +1% | White; counter at 70% |
| Body: video subtitles | Figtree | Bold 700 | 56–64 px | 1.15 | 0 | White, one word Orange |

**Display rules (Bricolage Grotesque)**

- 8 words or fewer, at most 3 lines. If it needs a 4th line, cut words; do not shrink below 120 px.
- Sentence case: capitalise only the first word, names and acronyms ("AI", "ChatGPT", "UMKM").
- Left-aligned, ragged right, except magazine-style covers, which are centred (see Layout library). Never justified.
- Highlight exactly one key word in Orange, preferably on the first or last line so the eye lands on it.
- Break lines by meaning, never mid-phrase, and never hyphenate an Indonesian word across lines.

**Body rules (Figtree)**

- Body text never exceeds about 40 words per slide or 6 lines.
- Keep line length around 32–40 characters; set body text boxes no wider than 820 px.
- Subtext (Bold 700) sits directly under the headline when the headline needs one supporting line. Use it at most once per slide.
- Emphasis inside body text is Figtree Bold in white, never orange, never italic, never underlined.

**Accent rules**

- Figtree Medium 500 is for small functional text only: the handle, slide counter ("2/7"), small labels such as "Prompt:" or "Sebelum / Sesudah".
- The orange accent in type comes from three places only: the one orange headline word, big step numbers, and label blocks (white Figtree Bold text, 48 px or larger, on an orange pill). In headlines orange is the text colour; in labels it is the block behind white text. Never both on one line, and never a block behind headline words.

**Hierarchy on any frame:** use no more than three type sizes at once (for example headline, body, handle). Bricolage is a variable font: its weight (200–800) and width (75–100%) can be set freely, so thin, bold and condensed versions count as the same font. Instrument Serif Italic appears only as the kicker line above the giant word on covers (96–110 px), as a short label above a headline on inner slides (48–64 px, for example "Langkah 1", "Istilahnya", "Coba hari ini"), or as one large quote on the pause slide; never for body text or the handle. The headline must be at least 2.5 times the size of the body text.

## 3. Layout rules

Text on inner slides lives on a left-aligned column inside 64 px side margins, the handle sits in the top-left corner label, and at least 35% of every frame stays empty.

**Spacing scale:** all distances are multiples of 8 px. Use only these steps: 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128 px.


### Carousel and feed post: 1080 × 1350 px (main format)

The cover and every inner slide are defined in section 6. The measurements that apply to all carousel slides:

| Zone | Measurement | What goes there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Side margins | 64 px left and right | Nothing, apart from the corner labels. |
| Corner labels | 44 px from the top, 52 px from the bottom, 64 px from the sides | @irsyadtawakal top-left, slide number (or series and number on the cover) top-right, "Geser →" bottom-right. The last slide uses "Simpan dulu" bottom-left and "Ikuti @irsyadtawakal" bottom-right. Figtree Medium, about 26 px, white at 85%. |
| Handle | top-left corner label | **@irsyadtawakal**, every slide, no exceptions. |
| Content area | between the corner labels | Headline, body and graphics. |

**Where text sits**

- Cover slide: one compact centred text block (section 6).
- Inner slides: left-aligned, following the five slide types in section 6.
- Gaps: headline to subtext 24–32 px, headline or subtext to body 48–64 px, between paragraphs 32 px, text block to graphic 64–96 px.

**Breathing room**

- Text covers no more than 40% of the canvas area, and 35–50% of the frame stays empty. Anchor the headline at the top and the body or graphic at the bottom, so the empty space sits in the middle.
- One text block and at most one graphic per slide. If it doesn't fit, split it into two slides.
- Nothing touches the canvas edge except a full-bleed background, a graphic that bleeds off on purpose, or a giant number.

### Reels, TikTok and Stories: 1080 × 1920 px

| Zone | Keep clear of text and key visuals |
| --- | --- |
| Top | 220 px (status bar, app header) |
| Bottom | 420 px (caption, music, buttons) |
| Right | 160 px (like, comment, share icons) |
| Left | 64 px |
| Safe box for text | x 64–920, y 220–1500 |

- **Handle on video:** inside the safe box, right edge at x 900, baseline at y 1480, Figtree Medium 34 px white, on screen for the full duration. Also written at the end of the caption.
- **Subtitles:** in the band y 1100–1400, one key word in orange at most. Subtitles may be centred; they are the only centred text in the system. Full caption spec (one line, 1–4 words) in Video Brain section 4.
- **Reel cover:** the profile grid shows only the middle 1080 × 1440 (y 240–1680), so keep the cover headline inside both that area and the safe box. Practical target: headline between y 300 and 1000, left-aligned at x 96.

### Square: 1080 × 1080 px

Same corner-label system as the carousel, 64 px side margins, measured from the new bottom edge. Headline 100–130 px.

### Wide thumbnail: 1280 × 720 px

Headline 4 words or fewer at 130–170 px. Keep the bottom-right 260 × 110 px free for the video timestamp, so the handle moves to the top-right: right edge 64 px from the edge, baseline 72 px from the top.

## 4. Composition principles

Every frame makes one point, read in a fixed order: the orange word first, then the headline, the visual, the body, and finally the handle.

### Core principles

1. **One idea per frame.** One headline, one supporting element. A second idea gets its own slide.
2. **Left-anchored and asymmetric.** Text hugs the left margin. Balance its weight with a visual or orange shape on the right or lower part of the frame. Never stack everything down the centre line. Magazine-style covers are the one exception: centred and symmetric.
3. **Contrast through scale, not decoration.** Make things important by making them bigger and orange, not by adding shadows, boxes or effects. One exception: a soft contact shadow under a photo cutout, object or device, matching the light direction, so it sits in the scene instead of looking pasted on. Text never gets a shadow.
4. **The orange word leads the eye.** Put it where the reader's eye lands first: the start of line 1 or the end of the last line. Nothing else orange should compete with it on the same frame, except one supporting shape such as the arrow.
5. **Empty space is part of the design.** If a frame looks full, remove something rather than shrinking it.
6. **Same family, every time.** Covers use the same layout so the profile grid reads as one brand at a glance.

### The curiosity loop, made visual

The voice opens a loop and pays it off. The layout does the same:

- **Cover** asks or teases, never answers. The answer is withheld visually: the corner label "Geser →" signals "swipe".
- **Inner slides** each reveal one piece. Steps get a serif italic label ("Langkah 1") or a giant orange number, then a short headline and body.
- **Payoff slide** shows the practical thing to try today, usually the prompt as plain text with an orange bar.
- **Last slide** closes with the call to action (follow or save) and, when promised, a tease of the next post.

**Default carousel structure (7–10 slides):** 1 hook cover → 1 second-hook slide that works on its own → 1 slide per step → 1 "coba hari ini" payoff slide → 1 call-to-action slide. Slide 2 must make sense to someone who never saw the cover: creators widely report that Instagram re-shows carousels from slide 2 to people who scrolled past (not officially confirmed). Write it as a short, bold claim of its own, for example "AI nggak bisa baca pikiranmu." A Socialinsider study of 22 million posts found 10-slide carousels had the highest engagement and that a swipe prompt helped, so aim for 7–10 slides and keep the swipe arrow.

### Cover composition

The cover has one layout, defined in section 6 (Cover: magazine style): a compact centred text block, a free image, four small corner labels. Do not use any other cover layout.

- **When Irsyad is the subject:** the image is made from his real photos as reference, so the face stays consistent. The subject may overlap the bottom of the giant word from in front, or sit just below the text block (section 6).
- **Orange echo:** one small orange object in the image (a mug, sticky note or phone case) can repeat the orange word. It is a prop, not an edit: never recolour clothing or skin.
- **How often the face appears:** about half of all covers show Irsyad; the rest use a free concept (an object, scene or metaphor). Never more than two Irsyad covers next to each other in the profile grid.

### Reusable components

- **Prompt block:** the prompt as plain text on indigo, Figtree Regular or Medium 36–42 px, white. Earlier sentences at 45% opacity; the newest sentence has a 6 px orange bar on its left edge; a caret at the end. No white card.
- **Screenshot card:** a real screenshot, cropped tight to the relevant part, corner radius 24 px, 2 px white outline at 15%. Never invented or edited to show results the tool didn't give.
- **Myth and fact:** the pause slide (section 6): the myth struck through at 40% opacity, then the fact with one large orange word. No red or green.
- **Step number:** Bricolage 800, orange, 180–240 px, bleeding off the left edge on a type slide; the step headline sits to the right or below.
- **Button or pill:** orange fill, fully rounded ends, text in Figtree Bold 48 px or larger, white. Used only for a call-to-action slide.
- **Icons:** simple line icons, white, stroke about 4 px with rounded ends, 64–96 px. At most one icon per slide may be orange.

### Photos and video framing

- Inner slides use designed graphics, not photos (section 6). Photos and AI images of Irsyad appear on covers, where imagery is free. AI-generated images of Irsyad are allowed (his decision, 6 Oct 2026) on two conditions: made from his real photos as reference so the face stays consistent, and used as illustration only, never as proof of an event or result that did not happen. Poses are warm and expressive: smiling, explaining with the hands, leaning in. Avoid stiff arms-crossed poses.
- Talking-head video: face in the upper-middle third, eyes around y 650–750 on 1080 × 1920, leaving the subtitle band clear.

## 5. Motifs

Motifs are the recurring elements that make a post recognisable as @irsyadtawakal before anyone reads the handle. The orange word and the hand-drawn orange mark are the two strongest; use them constantly.

| Motif | What it looks like | When it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The orange word | One word of the headline in Signal Orange, Bricolage 800 | Every headline, every frame |
| The hand-drawn orange mark | One loop, wavy underline, scribble strike or arrow in orange, drawn by hand, on a chat-objects graphic | Every graphic slide, exactly one |
| The swipe cue | "Geser →" corner label, bottom-right | Every slide except the last |
| The prompt block | Plain text on indigo, the newest sentence with a 6 px orange bar on its left, a caret at the end | Payoff slide, and tutorial slides that build a prompt |
| Big step number | Orange Bricolage 800 numeral, 180–240 px, bleeding off the left edge | Type slides for steps and lists |
| Slide label | Short Instrument Serif Italic line above a headline, 48–64 px | Image, window and colour-block slides |
| Myth and fact | Struck-through myth, then the fact with one large orange word | The pause slide |
| Typing caret | Thick vertical bar right after the last word of a cover line, a headline on an image slide, or a prompt | Covers, image slides, prompt block |
| Input bar (video only) | A chat input box holding the hook, with the typing caret; it returns empty at the end for the loop. Spec in Video Brain section 5 | First seconds and last frame of every video |
| Corner labels | @irsyadtawakal top-left, slide number top-right, "Geser →" bottom-right | Every frame |
| Subject over giant word | The cover subject overlaps the bottom of the giant word from in front | Covers, when the concept allows |
| Orange echo | One orange object in the cover image that repeats the orange word | Covers, when a prop fits naturally |

**Motif rules**

- Use no more than three motifs on one frame, not counting the orange word and the handle footer.
- Motifs are never resized or recoloured to "fit" a design. If it doesn't fit, change the layout.
- Never invent a new motif per post. A new motif is added to this table first, then used.

## 6. Layout library

The set of approved layouts, decided 6 Oct 2026. Pick one per slide; never invent a new layout for a single post. Sizes are for 1080 × 1350.

### Cover: magazine style (default)

Covers are generated in full, image and text together, in an AI image generator (ChatGPT Image or Nano Banana) from a structured JSON prompt. The Visual Brain locks only three things on a cover: the **layout**, the **typography** and the **handle**. Everything else, the scene, subject, mood and colours, is free and chosen per topic.

**Master reference cover:** the revised "Belajar AI dari mana?" (Fondasi AI #3, 6 Oct 2026, the staircase with an orange first step). Attach it to every cover generation with the line: *"Match the exact typography, text sizes, colours and layout of the attached reference cover; change only the image and the words."* This keeps the type consistent across the grid.

**Locked layout (1080 × 1350), revised 6 Oct 2026:**

- **Image first:** the text block covers no more than about 30% of the frame. The image carries the post.
- **Three text layers only, centred**, placed where the image is calmest (top by default, centre or bottom when the image asks for it):
  1. **Kicker line:** Instrument Serif Italic, 96–110 px, 2–4 words ("Belajar AI dari").
  2. **Giant word:** Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, condensed (width about 80%), about 360 px, line height 0.78, letter spacing about −1%, lowercase, one word of at most 10 characters. Never orange.
  3. **One line below:** Bricolage Grotesque Medium, about 46 px, one sentence of at most 8 words, holding the one orange word (Bricolage ExtraBold, #EF5F18) followed immediately by a thin cursor bar with no gap.
- **Subject:** either overlaps the bottom of the giant word from in front, or sits just below the text block. It never covers the kicker or the orange line.
- **Small corners:** top-left **@irsyadtawakal**, top-right series and number ("Fondasi AI #3"), bottom-left "Simpan dulu", bottom-right "Geser →". Figtree Medium, about 26 px, 85% opacity, about 44 px from the top and 52 px from the bottom, 64 px from the sides.
- **No hook line and no slide-number navigation on the cover.** The hook moves to the first line of the caption and to slide 2.
- **Keep the areas behind the text calm:** no busy objects, flying papers or high-contrast detail behind any text.

**Text colour rule:** all text is Deep Indigo #261A66 on light images and White #FFFFFF on dark images, never both on one cover, plus the one orange word. No other text colours.

**Two subject tracks, alternated in the grid:** *Irsyad* (from his real photos, or an AI image made from them) on about half of covers; *Free* (any object, scene or concept) on the rest. Never more than two Irsyad covers side by side.

### Cover imagery (free)

- Any scene, style or colour palette that fits the topic, as long as the upper part stays calm enough for the headline (sky, wall, studio backdrop or soft blur).
- The image carries a visual metaphor for the topic, not a literal illustration of a screen or app.
- The brand refusals in section 7 still apply: no robots, glowing brains, circuit boards, holograms or fear imagery.
- Spelling of every word is checked before posting; regenerate if any letter is wrong.
- Prompts are written with the json-prompt-generator skill (or the carousel-director skill), using the locked layout above as the `ui_elements` block.

### Inner slides: editorial rhythm (default)

Inner slides are no longer flat template cards. They follow the same editorial language as the cover (big type, serif italic labels, one orange word) and alternate between five slide types so the swipe has rhythm. Sizes are for 1080 × 1350; inner slides use 64 px side margins, like the cover.

**Hard rule: the cover image never appears on an inner slide.** No crop, no detail, no object or scene from the cover is reused. Every graphic is its own idea, drawn for that slide's point. The one exception is the CTA slide at the very end (see "CTA slide" below), which is not an inner content slide.

| Type | Looks like | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Image slide | Full-bleed designed graphic on an indigo gradient, text above it; serif italic kicker, giant headline, one supporting line | Slide 2 (the hook) and the "Coba hari ini" payoff |
| 2 · Window slide | Designed graphic in the top ~45% (full width, square corners), indigo below: serif italic label ("Langkah 1"), headline, one or two body lines | One step or one explanation |
| 3 · Type slide | Flat Deep Indigo, no image; giant type or a giant orange number bleeding off the left edge, headline and one line beside or below it. A list of three short rows with 15% white dividers also belongs here | Lists, key statements, updates ("Yang berubah / Yang tetap sama / Berguna buat") |
| 4 · Colour-block slide | Orange block (about 40% of the height, the one allowed exception to the 10% orange limit) with Deep Indigo text for the term; below it on indigo, an orange "=" and the everyday comparison | Explaining a term or analogy |
| 5 · Pause slide | Off-white #F4F2FF background, Deep Indigo text. Serif italic label in Deep Indigo, the myth struck through at 40% opacity, then the fact with one large bold orange word (120 px or more) | Myth versus fact, or one calm breather. Maximum 1 per carousel |

**Rhythm rules**

- Never two image slides in a row, and never more than two slides of the same type in a row.
- Slide 2 is an image slide or a type slide. The last content slide ("Coba hari ini") is an image slide or a type slide.
- A carousel of 7–8 slides uses at most 3 graphic slides; 9–10 slides at most 4. The rest are type, colour-block or pause slides.
- The growing prompt stays, but as plain text: earlier sentences at 45% opacity, the new sentence with a 6 px orange bar on its left edge and a caret at the end. No white card.
- Headline: sentence case, Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold at 80% width, 8 words or fewer, exactly one orange word. Labels in Instrument Serif Italic. Body in Figtree (Regular or Medium) at 36–44 px; the bottom line of a cover is Bricolage Medium, about 46 px.
- Orange text only on indigo or on a dark, calm part of an image, and only large and bold. On an orange block, text is Deep Indigo and large.
- Handle and counter stay in the corner labels: @irsyadtawakal top-left, slide number top-right, "Geser →" bottom-right. The last slide replaces it with "Simpan dulu" and "Ikuti @irsyadtawakal".

**Engagement structure (since 13 Oct 2026)**

Every carousel builds in four things that make people swipe to the end, save it and come back:

| Element | What it is | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Progress in the label | Step slides say where the reader is: "Langkah 1 dari 3" in the serif italic label | Only for step-by-step carousels. The count matches the steps exactly |
| Bridge line | One short line at the bottom of a step slide that teases the next slide: "Berikutnya: langkah yang paling sering dilewati" | Figtree Medium 30 px, white at 70%, 64 px from the left, about 120 px above the bottom edge (clear of the corner labels). Only a true tease of the next slide, never clickbait. At most on half of the slides |
| Contekan (recap) slide | A type slide just before "Coba hari ini" that puts the whole carousel on one screen: serif italic label "Contekan", then the 3–5 points as numbered rows with orange numbers and 15% white dividers | Rows of 3–8 words each, taken from the headlines. This is the slide people save, so it must stand alone |
| Proof slide (optional) | A window slide labelled "Contoh dari saya" with a real screenshot of Irsyad's own prompt and result, in a white rounded frame tilted 2–4° | Only with a real screenshot Irsyad sends, private data covered. Never a mock-up |
| CTA slide | The last slide continues the cover photo and asks the comment question, plus one line asking to save and follow (details below) | Replaces the series teaser (dropped on 23 Oct 2026). The cover still carries the series label top-right |

### CTA slide: the cover, a moment later (since 23 Oct 2026)

The last slide of every carousel with a generated cover closes the story the cover opened: the same scene a few seconds later.

- **Image:** a new image generated from the cover prompt (same person, setting, wardrobe, light, palette and camera), showing the next moment: the gift box opened, the answer found, the reaction after. Never a crop or copy of the cover. Made with the cover as an extra reference, so the face and scene match. Generated **without any text**, with the upper ~45% of the frame kept calm and empty for the text. Saved as `assets/cta.png`.
- **Text (rendered by `cta_slide` in `lib/slides.py`, not by the image generator):** centred like the cover. Serif italic kicker ("Giliran kamu"), then the comment question in Bricolage ExtraBold, about 96 px, 10 words or fewer, exactly one orange word, then one Figtree Medium line asking to save and follow ("Simpan biar nggak lupa. Follow buat fondasi berikutnya."). Text colour follows the cover's text colour rule.
- **The question:** about this carousel's topic, answerable from the reader's own life or work in a few words ("Kamu paling sering minta AI bikin apa?"). Never a keyword to type, never "tag teman" or "share kalau setuju", never a yes/no question. The Instagram caption closes with the same question.
- **Corners:** @irsyadtawakal top-left, slide number top-right, "Simpan dulu" bottom-left, "Ikuti @irsyadtawakal" bottom-right.
- It is not counted as a graphic slide in the rhythm limits.
- A carousel with a typographic cover (no generated image) ends with a type slide carrying the same text instead.

### Inner slides: designed graphics, "chat objects" style (signature)

Support visuals on inner slides are **designed graphics drawn in code**, not photos and not AI-generated images, so there is no image-generation step for inner slides; only the cover is generated. Since 13 Oct 2026 the style is **chat objects**: the everyday things of talking to AI (chat bubbles, app cards, sticky notes, the message input bar, a cursor), shown big, tilted and layered, with a real example sentence inside. Chosen over a hand-sketch style and a one-symbol minimal style because beginners recognise these objects instantly, the example inside the object teaches, and solid white objects stay readable at thumbnail size.

**Hard rule:** the cover image, and anything from it, never appears on an inner slide. Exception: the CTA slide at the end continues the cover scene (see "CTA slide").

**Signature rules (every graphic follows all of them):**

1. **Real objects, not abstract shapes.** Build the graphic from the object library below. Abstract pills, circles and venns are retired as the main graphic.
2. **A real example inside.** At least one object carries a concrete example sentence from the slide's point ("Pemilik warung kopi di Bogor", "Caption promo buat minggu ini"). The user's side of a chat can be an illustrative example; the AI's side is only shown as typing dots or a dashed empty bubble, never as an invented answer (section 7).
3. **Tilt and layer.** Objects tilt between 2° and 14°, overlap each other, and may run off the edge of the frame. Never all straight and centred. One object is the hero: solid White or Pause White #F4F2FF, larger and in front. The others are supporting: white at 14–30% opacity with a 3 px white outline, or Indigo Lift #3A2A9C.
4. **Big.** The graphic fills its zone (top ~45% on a window slide, the lower half on an image slide). No small graphic floating in empty space.
5. **Exactly one hand-drawn orange mark:** a loop, a wavy underline, a scribble strike or an arrow in #EF5F18, 6–9 px, round caps, slightly wobbly. It marks the one point of the slide. This is the brand's signature.
6. **One small white doodle is allowed** (a plus, a sparkle or a short tick) at 50–60% opacity, as a finishing touch. Never more than one.
7. **Text in objects:** Figtree (Medium for small labels at 26 px or more, Bold for the example sentence at 36–46 px), Deep Indigo on white objects, white on indigo objects. No handwriting font.
8. **Background:** Deep Indigo, or the subtle gradient #261A66 to #3A2A9C. No textures, 3D, drop shadows or glow. Depth comes only from overlap and opacity.
9. **Calm area for text:** the graphic stays in its zone and never sits behind the headline or body text.

**Dynamic layout (since 19 Oct 2026).** Inner graphic slides do not repeat one fixed template. Each graphic is drawn on a stage with a shape, and the composition is chosen from the content:

| Graphic shape (stage) | Compositions | Typical content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lebar, 1080 × 640 | **Atas** (graphic top, text below, Indigo Lift panel), **Bawah** (text top, graphic below, gradient; this is the image slide), **Tengah** (label and headline on top, graphic in the middle, body at the bottom) | Cards side by side, chat bubbles, an input bar |
| Tinggi, 520 × 1100 | **Samping kanan** or **Samping kiri** (graphic panel on one side, text column on the other, vertically centred) | A stack of sticky notes, a list going down, a phone screen |
| Kotak, 900 × 900 | **Kotak** (graphic centred above, text below) | One hero object |

Choosing rules (the library does this when the layout is "auto"):
- The hook slide and the "Coba hari ini" payoff use **Bawah** when the graphic is wide.
- Text over about 20 words goes to the composition with the most text room (**Atas**, or a side layout for tall graphics).
- Never the same composition twice in a row, never **Bawah** twice in a row, and side layouts alternate left and right.
- Type, colour-block, pause and Contekan slides keep their own layouts and break up the rhythm.

**Text that fits itself.** Headline and body sizes adjust to the length of the text: short headlines (3 words or fewer) may grow up to about 1.4× the normal size, long ones shrink down to 60 px, and the body stays between 36 and 44 px. Nothing is ever cut off; if a text still does not fit at the minimum, shorten the text instead of shrinking further.

## 7. What this is not

This brand is not "generic AI content": no glowing tech clichés, no clutter, no hype. If an output contains any of the following, it is wrong.

**Colour**

- No gradients, except the one subtle indigo gradient on graphic slides (section 6), and never the purple-to-blue "AI gradient". No glows, neon, lens flares or light leaks.
- No colours outside the three and the two indigo tints in section 1, including red and green for right/wrong, blue for links, or black. Screenshots and cover imagery are the only exceptions.
- No orange body text, no orange small text, no orange text on white (except the one large word on the pause slide), no orange block behind headline words (the colour-block slide holds a single term in Deep Indigo on orange).

**Type**

- No fourth font: no script, handwritten, monospace (not even for prompts), other serifs or "techy" display fonts.
- No ALL CAPS headlines, no centred headline blocks (except the cover text block), no justified text.
- No italic except Instrument Serif Italic in its three allowed roles (cover kicker, short slide label, pause quote); no text outlines or strokes, no drop shadows, no 3D or glossy text effects.
- No headline over 8 words, and never more than one orange word in it.

**Imagery**

- No robots, glowing brains, circuit boards, binary code, "Matrix" rain, holograms or humanoid AI faces.
- No stock photos of people pointing at screens, handshakes or generic office scenes. No colour photos of people on inner slides (covers are free), and no halftone, grain, torn-paper or grunge photo effects.
- No AI-generated images of other real people. AI images of Irsyad only under the conditions in "Photos and video framing", never as proof of real events or results.
- No fake interfaces presented as real, invented chatbot answers or edited screenshots. Show only real outputs. Chat objects on inner slides are clearly illustrations: the user's example message is allowed, the AI's side is only typing dots or an empty bubble.
- No made-up charts, numbers or statistics as graphics. A figure appears only with a real source.

**Mood and layout**

- No fear visuals: warning signs, skulls, red alerts or "AI takes over" imagery.
- No clutter: no busy backgrounds, textures or patterns, no more than one graphic group per slide, no emoji or decorative stickers. Chat objects are the only illustration style on inner slides.
- No meme templates or Impact-style caption text.
- No watermark or logo other than **@irsyadtawakal**, and never a slide without it. Exception: official tool logos on app cards when the slide names those tools (section 6).
- No clickbait visuals: the cover never promises something the slides don't deliver.

## Pre-flight checklist

Run this on every frame before delivering it. One "no" means fix it first.

- [ ] Carousel: a Contekan slide before "Coba hari ini", step labels show progress, bridge lines are true teases, and the last slide is the CTA slide (the cover scene a moment later, one real comment question, save and follow line).
- [ ] Cover: layout, typography, text colour rule and handle are correct (imagery is free). Inner slide: it is one of the five types in section 6, the background is Deep Indigo `#261A66`, an orange block, an off-white pause slide (max 1) or a chat-objects graphic (tilted and layered, one solid hero object with a real example inside, exactly one hand-drawn orange mark), and the cover image appears nowhere (the CTA slide at the end is the only exception).
- [ ] Rhythm: no two image slides in a row, no more than two of the same type in a row, graphic slides within the limit (3 for 7–8 slides, 4 for 9–10).
- [ ] Only Indigo, White and Orange (plus the off-white pause tint) appear, apart from real screenshots and designed graphics.
- [ ] Only Bricolage Grotesque and Figtree are used, plus Instrument Serif Italic for a cover kicker, a short slide label or a pause-slide quote.
- [ ] Headline: 8 words or fewer, sentence case, left-aligned (centred only on covers), exactly one orange word.
- [ ] Orange appears only as large bold text or shapes, never on white.
- [ ] Everything sits inside the margins or video safe box; carousel corner labels hold only the handle, slide number and "Geser →" (or "Simpan dulu" and "Ikuti @irsyadtawakal" on the last slide).
- [ ] **@irsyadtawakal** is visible on this frame (top-left corner label on carousel slides; the video safe position on video).
- [ ] At least 35% of the frame is empty; one idea, one visual.
- [ ] Nothing from "What this is not" is present.
- [ ] The cover's promise is actually paid off by the end.
