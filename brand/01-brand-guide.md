## Colours

Three colours only: deep indigo as the base, white for text, orange as the spark.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use for | Share of design |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark | Deep Indigo | #261A66 | Main background | About 60% |
| Light | White | #FFFFFF | Body text, headlines, breathing space | About 30% |
| Accent | Signal Orange | #EF5F18 | Key words in headlines, buttons, highlights | About 10% |

- Default background is Deep Indigo. White text on indigo is the standard look.
- Orange is only for large, bold text or shapes. Never use it for small text or long sentences.
- Never put orange text on a white background, except one large bold word on the single off-white pause slide. White text on an orange button must be large and bold.
- Two tints of Deep Indigo are allowed only where the Visual Brain specifies them: #3A2A9C (a subtle gradient on graphic slides) and #F4F2FF (the pause slide background). They never appear as text colour.

## Fonts

Three Google Fonts: [Bricolage Grotesque](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque) for headlines, [Figtree](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Figtree) for everything else, and Instrument Serif Italic as an editorial accent on covers.

| Use | Font | Weight | Size on a 1080 x 1350 px post |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Headline | Bricolage Grotesque | ExtraBold (800) | 120 to 150 px, tight line spacing (1.0) |
| Subtext | Figtree | Bold (700) | 48 to 56 px |
| Body | Figtree | Regular (400) or Medium (500) | 36 to 44 px, relaxed line spacing (1.4) |
| Handle and small labels | Figtree | Medium (500) | 30 to 34 px |
| Cover kicker line | Instrument Serif | Italic (400) | 96 to 110 px |
| Short slide label (inner slides) | Instrument Serif | Italic (400) | 48 to 64 px |

- Headlines: 8 words or fewer, sentence case (not ALL CAPS), left-aligned on inner slides. Covers use a compact centred text block (see the Visual Brain).
- Highlight one key word per headline in orange.
- Instrument Serif Italic is the third font (added 6 Oct 2026), used only for the kicker line on covers, short labels above headlines on inner slides, and large quotes on pause slides; never for body text or the handle. Never add a fourth font.

## Voice

Open a curiosity loop, then pay it off in plain, casual Indonesian that anyone can follow.

**Do this**

1. Open with a curiosity loop: hint that there is an answer, hold it back, and reveal it later in the same post or in the promised next post.
2. Keep it short and simple: one idea per sentence, address the reader as "kamu", use everyday comparisons, and explain any tech term right away.
3. Always close with something practical the viewer can try today.

**Don't do this**

1. Don't clickbait. Never promise something the content doesn't deliver.
2. Don't make up numbers or statistics. Only use figures from a real source.
3. Don't use jargon, stiff formal language, heavy slang, or fear-based hype like "AI will take your job".

**Example lines**

- "Kebanyakan orang pakai ChatGPT dengan cara yang salah, dan kesalahannya ada di kalimat pertama yang mereka ketik."
- "Ada satu kata yang bikin jawaban AI langsung jauh lebih bagus. Hampir nggak ada yang pakai."
- "Saya kasih perintah yang sama ke dua orang. Hasilnya beda jauh. Bedanya cuma satu hal kecil."
- "Langkah ketiga ini yang paling sering dilewati, padahal justru di situ kuncinya."
- "Di post berikutnya saya bongkar cara bikin AI menulis persis pakai gaya bahasamu. Follow biar nggak ketinggalan."
