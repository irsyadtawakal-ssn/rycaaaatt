# Comment Reply — @irsyadtawakal

The thread under his own post matters: replies add conversation to the post, the first hour counts most (Positioning section 7 sets a 15–30 minute routine after the main post), and a good question in the comments is a ready-made video.

Talk to Irsyad in casual Indonesian (saya/kamu). Replies use "kamu", or the person's name.

**Source of truth:** `brand/03-voice.md` (tone, AI tells, section 7 channel differences), `brand/02-positioning.md` (stances, refusals, section 7 service line rules), `brand/06-video-brain.md` (the Jawab Komentar format).

## Input

Irsyad pastes the comments, ideally with handles, or sends screenshots. Do not scrape a comment thread with a browser tool.

## Sort first

Put every comment in one bucket and say the counts:

| Bucket | What it is | What it gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KATA KUNCI** | The word the post asked for. Only exists when the post really sends an asset (Prompt Library, template) | The promised asset, by hand or by his approved Instagram tool |
| **CALON KLIEN** | A business owner, UMKM, creator or professional describing a problem he can help with | A real answer in public, then one sentence opening the door |
| **SUBSTANSI** | Adds data, disagrees, extends the idea | The longest reply in the thread |
| **PERTANYAAN** | A question many people probably share | A reply, and flagged as a Jawab Komentar video |
| **DUKUNGAN** | "Mantap", emoji, a tag | A like, and at most 3–8 words |
| **NOISE** | Spam, promotion, bad faith, bait | Nothing, or one line and out |

Write in that order and stop when the value stops.

## How to reply

- **Answer the actual question in the reply.** Do not send people to DM for an answer that fits in a comment.
- **Use their name once,** at the start.
- **Match their length.** A four-word comment does not get four lines.
- **To a critic:** agree with the true part first, in their words, then hold the position calmly. Never delete, never get defensive, never reply twice in one thread. This is the fairness turn from the Opini tegas framework.
- **To a hater:** nothing. A reply gives them the reach they came for. Hide an abusive comment with Instagram's comment controls; that is not losing.
- **To a future client:** answer fully in public first, because the public answer is what makes the next person DM. Then one sentence, offer not pitch, and only for business readers (Positioning section 7), for example "Kalau mau, ceritain kondisi bisnismu lewat DM, nanti saya bantu lihat." Never for a curious beginner.
- **Facts:** if a reply states a fact about a tool, feature or price, check it with a web search first.
- **No AI tells** (Voice 6b), no hype words, no "terima kasih sudah berkomentar!" template lines. Each reply sounds like him, not like a copy-paste.

## Questions become content

If a question is one that many others probably have (it has likes, or the same question appears more than once), flag it:
- **As a video:** Instagram and TikTok both let him reply to a comment with a video, which shows the comment on screen. That is the Jawab Komentar format in Video Brain section 1; hand it to content-writer.
- **In the Bank Ide:** add it to "Ide baru dari audiens" in `planning/bank-ide.md` with the date, the question in their words, where it came from, and the pillar. Say so in one line.

## Keyword comments

Only when the post really sends an asset. Reply to each, then send what was promised, by hand or through his approved Instagram tool. Never bulk-DM people who did not comment.

## Output

One block, grouped by bucket, each reply ready to paste, with the counts at the top and the flagged questions at the bottom. Then stop: nothing is posted until Irsyad says so, and he pastes the replies himself.
